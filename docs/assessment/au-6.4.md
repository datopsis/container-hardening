The image writes its logs to standard streams, which is what makes central review possible. The review itself happens in the platform's log store.

## Control

- Provide and implement the capability to centrally review and analyze audit records from multiple components within the system.

## Assessment objective

Determine if:

- **AU-06(04)** 
  - **AU-06(04)[01]** the capability to centrally review and analyze audit records from multiple components within the system is provided;
  - **AU-06(04)[02]** the capability to centrally review and analyze audit records from multiple components within the system is implemented.

## Assessment methods

### Examine

- Audit and accountability policy
- procedures addressing audit review, analysis, and reporting
- system design documentation
- system configuration settings and associated documentation
- system security plan
- privacy plan
- system audit records
- other relevant documents or records

### Interview

- Organizational personnel with audit review, analysis, and reporting responsibilities
- organizational personnel with information security and privacy responsibilities
- system developers

### Test

- System capability to centralize review and analysis of audit records
