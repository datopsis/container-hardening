Maintenance of the system is performed by the organization. An image is not maintained in place; it is replaced.

## Control

- Obtain maintenance support and/or spare parts for [*Assignment: system components*] within [*Assignment: time period*] of failure.

## Assessment objective

Determine if:

- **MA-06** maintenance support and/or spare parts are obtained for [*Assignment: system components*] within [*Assignment: time period*] of failure.

## Assessment methods

### Examine

- Maintenance policy
- procedures addressing system maintenance
- service provider contracts
- service-level agreements
- inventory and availability of spare parts
- system security plan
- other relevant documents or records

### Interview

- Organizational personnel with system maintenance responsibilities
- organizational personnel with acquisition responsibilities
- organizational personnel with information security responsibilities
- system/network administrators

### Test

- Organizational processes for ensuring timely maintenance
