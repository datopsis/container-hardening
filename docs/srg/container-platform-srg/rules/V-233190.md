# SRG-APP-000383-CTR-000910

**All nonessential, unnecessary, and unsecure DoW ports, protocols, and services must be disabled in the container platform.**

## Discussion

To properly offer services to the user and to orchestrate containers, the container platform may offer services that use ports and protocols that best fit those services. The container platform, when offering the services, must only offer the services on ports and protocols authorized by the DoW.

To validate that the services are using only the approved ports and protocols, the organization must perform a periodic scan/review of the container platform and disable functions, ports, protocols, and services deemed to be unneeded or nonsecure.

## Check

Review the container platform configuration to determine if services or capabilities presently on the information system are required for operational or mission needs.

If additional services or capabilities are present on the system, this is a finding.

## Fix

Configure the container platform to only utilize secure ports and protocols required for operation that have been accepted for use as per the Ports, Protocols, and Services Category Assignments List (CAL) from DISA (PPSM).
