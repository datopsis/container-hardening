# SRG-APP-000700-DB-000100

**The DBMS must disable accounts when the accounts have expired.**

## Discussion

Disabling expired, inactive, or otherwise anomalous accounts supports the concepts of least privilege and least functionality, which reduce the attack surface of the system.

## Check

Verify the DBMS is configured to disable accounts when the accounts have expired.

If the DBMS is not configured to disable accounts when the accounts have expired, this is a finding.

## Fix

Configure the DBMS to disable accounts when the accounts have expired.
