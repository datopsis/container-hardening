Contingency planning, backup, and recovery are planned and exercised by the organization operating the system. An image holds no state of its own; its data lives on volumes the deployment provides.

## Control

- Identify potential accessibility problems to alternate processing sites in the event of an area-wide disruption or disaster and outlines explicit mitigation actions.

## Assessment objective

Determine if:

- **CP-07(02)** 
  - **CP-07(02)[01]** potential accessibility problems to alternate processing sites in the event of an area-wide disruption or disaster are identified;
  - **CP-07(02)[02]** explicit mitigation actions to address identified accessibility problems are outlined.

## Assessment methods

### Examine

- Contingency planning policy
- procedures addressing alternate processing sites
- contingency plan
- alternate processing site
- alternate processing site agreements
- primary processing site agreements
- system security plan
- other relevant documents or records

### Interview

- Organizational personnel with contingency plan alternate processing site responsibilities
- organizational personnel with system recovery responsibilities
- organizational personnel with information security responsibilities
